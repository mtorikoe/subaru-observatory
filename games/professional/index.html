AO3K — Facility Adaptive Optics
AO3K is Subaru's upgraded facility AO system with 3228 actuators. It provides extreme-AO correction for all instruments on the IR Nasmyth platform: IRCS, IRD, and SCExAO.

AO3K reduces wavefront error from ~600 nm rms (raw atmosphere) to ~180 nm rms — concentrating the PSF enough for coronagraphy to work.
Command
ao3k-perform — apply correction, update PALILA

Requires close-adaptive-opetics-loop first.
Skipping AO3K reduces image quality and disables the real-image reward.
SCExAO — Extreme AO & Coronagraphy
SCExAO receives the AO3K-corrected beam and applies fine wavefront correction with its 2000-actuator DM, reaching below 30 nm rms.

It then applies coronagraphy — a PIAA focal-plane mask + Lyot stop — revealing faint companions hidden in stellar glare.
Commands
scexao-pyramid-wave-front-sensor-on — optional hidden bonus
scexao-perform-dm — DM correction (must come first)
scexao-perform-coronagraph — insert PIAA mask
scexao-perform-lyot — insert Lyot stop
scexao-auto-complete-perform — shortcut, no bonus

Coronagraph and Lyot may be done in either order after DM.
CHARIS — Coronagraphic High Angular Resolution IFS
CHARIS is a lenslet-based cryogenic integral field spectrograph behind SCExAO. It images exoplanets across J+H+K bands (1.15–2.37 µm, R~18) simultaneously, producing a 3-D data cube.

Targets brighter than H~1 require the internal ND3 filter, restricting use to broadband mode.
Commands (in order)
charis-prism-disperse — separate spectral components
charis-reconstruct-raw-image — rebuild data cube

Both steps must complete to unlock the final image.
VAMPIRES — Visible Aperture Masking Polarimetric Imager
VAMPIRES is a visible-light, dual-camera instrument on the SCExAO platform. It uses non-redundant aperture masking interferometry and polarimetry to image resolved structures at very small separations.
Commands
vampire-split-polarization — both channels simultaneously
vampire-h-alpha — Hα channel → VCAM1
vampire-continuum — continuum channel → VCAM2

Use split-polarization, or h-alpha + continuum together. Both approaches are valid.
