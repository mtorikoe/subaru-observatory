① AO3K — Facility AO
Subaru's 188-actuator facility adaptive optics system. Performs initial wavefront correction so the PSF is concentrated enough for coronagraphy.
② SCExAO — High-Order AO
Wavefront sensor — reading residuals
Control system — computing correction
Deformable mirror — 2000 actuators
③ Coronagraph Mask
PIAA focal-plane mask placed over the now-sharp PSF core. Blocks direct starlight by ~10⁴×. Only effective after AO sharpening.
④ Lyot Stop
Pupil-plane annular mask suppressing diffracted light from the coronagraph edges. Final step before companion detection.
